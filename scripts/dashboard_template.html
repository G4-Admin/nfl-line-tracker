Reading the line

A spread of -3.5 means the home team is laying 3.5. The number shown is the median across every US book returning a spread that day.

Movement

toward the home team · toward the visitor. Every pill also names the team, so color is never doing the work alone.

Key numbers

NFL margins cluster on 3 and 7. A line crossing either is flagged, because that is where a half point actually changes what a ticket is worth.

Which baseline

Movement is measured from the consensus on the Tuesday that opens each game's own week — after Monday night resolves the previous week and books repost. A game whose week hasn't arrived shows its September lookahead number instead and is marked pre-week; drift from a lookahead line is the market learning the teams, not news, so it is never counted as movement.

What context is shown

Quarterbacks at any designation, other premium positions only when out or doubtful, and wind at outdoor venues inside ten days. Tagged new if the designation changed in the last three days.

What is deliberately absent

No ATS records, no points per game, no rest or bye angles. Season-long stats are already inside the line before you see it, and the bye-week edge has tested flat since the 2011 CBA. Only things that can still change the number appear here.
